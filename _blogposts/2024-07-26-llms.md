**Update July 25th, 2024: Benchmarking Large Language Models 🎖**

We benchmarked the latest **#LLMs**: Llama 3.1 (from Meta), Mistral-Large (Mistral AI), GPT-4o-mini (OpenAI).

**Findings:**

1. Llama 3.1 405B as good as GPT-4, Claude 3.5 👆
2. GPT-4o-mini: has drastic performance reduction 👇
3. Mistral-Large: good alternative but far behind L3.1-405B

Open-source has caught up to commercial models.

The leaderboard 🏁 is available here: [huggingface.co/spaces/colab-potsdam/clem-leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/colab-potsdam/clem-leaderboard)
